Solid State Physics

Conductor: Abolfazl Mahdaei


In this circle, we will study and learn solid state physics. The goal is to become familiar with and acquire the basic foundations necessary for the condensed matter physics track. We will also make use of prior knowledge from quantum mechanics and statistical mechanics, allowing us to see more clearly how these subjects find application in condensed matter systems. The sessions will start from fundamental topics and will roughly cover the equivalent of Solid State Physics I as taught in universities.

This circle will also be interactive: interested students will present different sections of the book in each session. Further details about the format, presentations, and references will be given in the introductory “session zero.”

The topics of the circle will follow the order of the table of contents of the main reference book, but we may also bring in material from other sources. Any changes or updates will be announced in the group:


Reference:The primary text we will follow is Oxford Solid State Basics by Steven Simon — a clear and accessible book for beginning this subject. As mentioned, additional material will occasionally be drawn from other sources, such as David Tong’s lecture notes. Supplementary resources will also be shared in the group. We will also have problem-solving sessions, where we will work through exercises together.


Prerequisites:*Basic concepts of classical mechanics and electromagnetism *Thermodynamics and statistical mechanics *Familiarity with quantum mechanics
